## Exact photographic decisions before candidate outcomes

Analyze only the decoded/oriented/profile-converted original with unchanged scene-v2 recognition and limits. WB is an explicit internal `white_balance=True`/CLI `--white-balance` candidate requiring scene-aware protection, pipeline version `scene-aware-natural-v3`; scene-policy caps/version remain v2. After frozen review passes, the existing default-off scene checkbox dispatches both flags and its upload cache uses the new pipeline version. Direct scene-aware calls without WB, old-default and DNG routes are unchanged. Skip WB before model inference for unavailable scene evidence, document preservation, source display-luma median<.06, or night/sunset within .02 of the top lighting similarity in either view. These conservative mood rules do not establish true illuminant or intent.

Preview follows the author's aspect-preserving two-stage RGB Pillow BICUBIC resize, max side384, dimensions rounded then extended to multiples16; reject extreme aspect ratio>16. Model input is display-sRGB float32 RGB/255 without normalization. Check finite/shape, clip prediction to[0,1], keep floats (unlike the reference PIL8 intermediate). Fit the author's11-term polynomial `[r,g,b,rg,rb,gb,r²,g²,b²,rgb,1]` to this input/output using NumPy least squares in normalizedRGB, rcond1e-6. Reject rank<11 or condition>1e6. Normalized basis avoids raw8-bit conditioning; no new learning or expert pixels enter fitting. This per-image fit transfers predicted colors, not spatial texture, to the full-resolution source in128-row chunks.

Source neutral candidates require .15<=displayY<=.90 and RGB max-min<=.22; require>=128pixels and>=2%of preview. Compare chroma `RGB-Y`: mean absolute chroma must decrease at least10% under the learned prediction, source mean chroma>=.01 and mean correction vector magnitude>=.003. Require at least two2x2quadrants containing>=64candidate pixels; every supported quadrant mean correction must have cosine>=.5 with the overall mean correction. No support/disagreement is a skip, not permission to normalize an uncertain warm light. These thresholds are provisional fixed heuristics, not measured model confidence or neutrality truth.

On accepted WB, use only polynomial chroma difference at full resolution, removing its luminance component. Blend at .5 and bound each pixel's largest absolute channel change to .04. A continuous BT.601 YCbCr skin-color candidate weight uses Cb ramp70→85and135→125, Cr ramp125→140and185→170 (8-bit codes); its full-weight cap is .025, smoothly interpolating to .04 elsewhere. This is a color-proxy restraint, not skin/face detection or demographic validation. Further scale correction to the current pixel gamut/headroom: no new full-channel255positions, preserve original all-black; round to8bit. Require zero new black/full positions, luminance change<=.501codes and actual channel change<=10.501codes. Reject malformed/nonfinite mappings or guard failures to the exact source, record reasons. Asset failure skips WB and reproduces scene-v2 pixel processing; scene failure reproduces old-default processing.

Apply this WB before the unchanged restrained LUT/tone and independently selected shadow lift. Existing scene caps/recognition remain fixed; final original-based endpoint guard still restores decoded original/base/zero shadow consistently and marks any WB rollback. Source statistics/scene recognition/shadow selection use original, not expert targets. Cached zero-strength remains exact decoded original; sliders never accumulate changes.
