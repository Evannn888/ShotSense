# Small scene-grouped low-light curve experiment

Fixed before new sample extraction/training, 2026-10-02. This is a development experiment with separate rendered-image targets, not RAW Catalog regression or production promotion.

Use the same verified LOL archive as the 24-pair pilot. Retain those 24 inspected pairs as training/development only. Add 24 previously unextracted `our485` pairs using seed 20261004 and matching sorted filenames, independent of image content/quality. Official `eval15` pixels remain untouched. All data keep existing RGB/bit-depth/dimension/ICC checks and quality-95 JPEG conversion. Archive and member hashes are fixed.

Before training, review input/reference contact sheets and low/reference perceptual-hash candidate pairs using the existing grouping helper. Reference images may help identify scenes but never become prediction inputs or features. Record explicit reviewed related-scene groups. Any group containing an earlier inspected pilot ID is forced into training. Shuffle remaining groups with seed 20261005 to assign approximately 16 pairs to validation, keeping entire groups together. All other pairs train. If related-scene grouping leaves fewer than eight validation groups, stop before training. Grouping remains conservative/incomplete; results are small development evidence, not independent test acceptance.

Candidate: 19 source-only display-sRGB statistics (RGB mean/std, five luminance quantiles, eight-bin luminance histogram), train-only standardization, MLP 19→32→3 and three bounded per-channel gamma values `exp(tanh(logits) * log(1/0.15))`. Apply channel-wise `y=x**gamma` in encoded sRGB, identity initialization. Output strength interpolates original and adjusted pixels, with exact zero-strength identity. Monotonic channel curves preserve float black/white endpoints, but can change hue and round near-white pixels to 255. There is no denoiser, spatial mask, RAW parameter interpretation or complete ISP.

CPU, one Torch thread, AdamW learning rate 0.003/weight decay 0.0001, batch 16, maximum 100 epochs, patience 15, seeds 42/43/44. Train on proportion-preserving maximum-edge-96 JPEG inputs and normal-light references with RGB MSE. Select by validation MSE including epoch-zero identity. Evaluate selected models at native image resolution, without target inputs or target-aware tuning. Save train-only statistics, checkpoints, histories, split/groups, source/member/model hashes and per-image native metrics. Three seeds share the same split. No hyperparameter grid.

Baselines: native decoded-JPEG identity, existing experimental JPEG estimates/v3, fixed +1 EV/v3, and a learned **constant** three-channel gamma curve using the same source/target loss, bounds and optimizer. Fit constant curves only on training pixels; use the same validation checkpoint criterion. Per-photo target-informed fits from the earlier diagnostic are not deployable baselines.

Small expansion screen: mean per-image validation PSNR at least 0.5 dB above both experimental-v3 and learned constant baselines, at least two of three seeds better than both, and mean new full-channel fraction at most 0.5% of pixels. Require finite training, monotonic/endpoints/identity/strength checks, NumPy/Torch parity and offline review for color/noise failures. Even passing these screens only permits a larger grouped development experiment; unseen personal-photo review, independent test evaluation and deployment gates remain incomplete. Inspect at most the first eight validation pairs chosen by sorted ID before predictions, plus summary metrics for every validation pair. Do not change thresholds after results.
